# Brand-book page specifications

The per-page content + layout contract. Abstracted from a real agency brand book — structure and
rules only, no source brand's specifics. Each page is a 16:9 landscape box. Build the pages the
brief needs (see the required/recommended/optional tags in `../SKILL.md`), in this order.

**Two rules that override everything on these pages (see `QUALITY.md`):**
- **Wherever "the logo" appears, use the REAL supplied logo file as an image — never reconstruct,
  redraw, or recolor it.** If it doesn't contrast with a ground, change the ground or frame it in a
  light tile; never alter the mark.
- **Specimens use the REAL embedded fonts** (from the font study, see `fonts.md`) — never fallbacks.

**Shared chrome (every inner page):**
- Top-left eyebrow: the brand name in the **accent color**, small, **uppercase and letter-spaced**.
- Below it (tight stack): the page title, **in the display face**, dark/neutral.
- **Generous, consistent** outer margin; everything hangs off the same left-margin line.
- **Confident scale + whitespace** — specimens large, pages breathing. One idea per page.
- Spec pages sit on the **page ground (white/cream)**; cover and divider pages may use a **full
  brand-color ground**.

---

## 1. Cover  *[required]*
- A brand-color ground — but **choose a ground the real logo reads against.** If the logo is a dark
  mark, do NOT put it on a dark ground where it vanishes; use a light/cream or accent ground (or
  frame the real logo in a light tile). Never recolor the mark to fix contrast.
- The **real logo**, large, centered-ish or upper area.
- "Brand Guidelines" (or "<Brand> Brand Guidelines") as the title, in the display face.
- Optional: the tagline in the script/accent face, if the brand has one.
- Footer row: `year / v.N` on the left; author / agency + URL on the right, small.

## 2. Logo  *[required]*
- Clean ground. **The real primary logo**, centered, at comfortable size. Nothing else.

## 3. Exclusion zone (clearspace)  *[required]*
- Tinted ground. Logo centered with a **clearspace box** drawn around it, the margin derived from
  a logo element (e.g. the cap-height or a letter width) — show the measuring unit as small boxed
  markers at the corners/midpoints. State the rule: "minimum clear space = X on all sides."

## 4. Logo guidelines / construction  *[recommended]*
- Tinted ground. Logo with **cap-height** and **baseline** guides labeled; optional proportion
  marks (dotted circles/lines) showing how letterforms are constructed/aligned.

## 5. Color versions  *[required]*
- White ground. The logo shown in its approved lockups, grouped and labeled:
  **Primary versions** (on light + on dark), **Secondary versions** (on accent + on near-black),
  **Achromatic versions** (black, white). Each on its own color tile.

## 6. Brand colors  *[required]*
- White ground. Full-height vertical swatches (typically 4–6), light → dark.
- Each swatch footer lists values, label-aligned: **RGB**, **# (HEX)**, and **Pantone** + **CMYK**
  when print is in scope. HEX is always present.

## 7. Logo icon  *[recommended]*
- White ground. App-icon treatments: the mark inside a **circle** and a **squircle/rounded-square**
  at large size, plus one or two **tiny** sizes to prove legibility. On the accent/tint.

## 8. Minimum size  *[recommended]*
- Tinted ground. The logo at its smallest allowed size with a dimension line and the value
  (print: mm; screen: px). State "do not reproduce smaller than X."

## 9. Main typeface  *[required]*
- White ground. The **primary font name** set very large as a specimen. A short styled line + a
  paragraph of body copy to show it in use. Source URL (e.g. the foundry/fontshare link), small,
  top-right.

## 10. Secondary typeface  *[recommended]*
- White ground. Same treatment for the **secondary font** (often a contrasting serif/italic).
  Large specimen + a short paragraph. Source URL top-right.

## 11. Typographic hierarchy  *[required]*
- Tinted ground. Two columns: left = the **role list** (Title, Subtitle, Subheading, Body) each
  labeled with its face + weight; right = a **live specimen** of each role stacked, showing real
  size relationships and a paragraph of body text.

## 12. Typographic hierarchy — color variations  *[optional]*
- White ground with color tiles. The same hierarchy specimen repeated across each brand ground
  (white, tint, accent, dark) to prove legibility of each role on each surface.

## 13. The signature move  *[recommended]*
- The brand's **one bold move**, shown large across a 2×2 or banded grid on different grounds
  (incl. one over a photo). This is the page that makes the brand feel *authored* — e.g. a
  distinctive letterform treatment, a graphic device, a motif. (In the reference book: the logo's
  italic letter threaded into real words.)

## 14. The signature move — guidelines  *[optional]*
- Numbered construction of the signature (01 base → 02 the move → 03 result), with cap-height /
  baseline guides so it can be reproduced consistently.

## 15. Interactive / UI text elements  *[optional — web brands]*
- The brand's **buttons and links** across the four grounds (white, tint, accent, dark), showing
  primary / secondary / tertiary (outline) levels and their states. Bridges brand → UI.

## 16. Logo composition rules  *[recommended]*
- The logo anchored within **Horizontal / Square / Vertical** format blocks, showing correct
  placement (typically a bottom corner with consistent inset).

## 17. Logo composition — guidelines  *[optional]*
- The same three formats with the logo's **safe-area / inset construction** drawn.

## 18. Logo placement  *[recommended]*
- The logo placed on **photography** in vertical + horizontal formats — on a solid brand bar at
  the bottom, or directly on the image.

## 19. Logo placement — guidelines  *[optional]*
- The same with diagonal safe-area guides / inset marks showing the construction.

## 20. Logo on background image  *[recommended]*
- The logo set **directly over imagery** (vertical + horizontal), proving contrast/legibility;
  state the rule for choosing the on-image logo color.

## 21. Logo misuse  *[required]*
- A grid of **don'ts**, each with a cross/strike: don't recolor off-palette, don't stretch/skew,
  don't rotate, don't add effects, don't place on low-contrast grounds, don't crowd the clearspace,
  don't rearrange letterforms.

## 22. Co-branding  *[optional]*
- Approved **partner lockups**: `<Brand> | Partner` with the correct divider, spacing, and
  relative sizing; horizontal and stacked variants.
