Display typeface

Display
A styled line in the display face.
A warm, high-contrast editorial serif with drawn italics — it carries headlines and the brand voice, and echoes the serif of the KF monogram. Used for everything that sets the tone.
Brand
Body typeface
foundry.com/body-serif
Body
Built for long-form reading on screen, with an optical-size axis and real italics. It keeps the essays — fitness, nutrition, the mental-health work — calm, warm, and genuinely readable.
The body italic handles emphasis and asides, staying in the same voice as the display face so the page reads as one hand.
