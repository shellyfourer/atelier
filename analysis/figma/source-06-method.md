## Source Method — Brand-Dominant High-Chroma Storefront (Figma, full-layer)

A storefront design file whose organizing idea is a **single strong master brand identity**, not a product taxonomy and not neutral app chrome. Analyzed across five layers (color, typography, component architecture, page composition, page anatomy). Its distinguishing move is that **color is spent as dominant structure rather than as trim** — one saturated brand hue is the primary surface, deployed as large full-bleed blocks that also do the page-segmentation work.

### What this source teaches that the prior four did not

**Color — a fourth register (BRAND-DOMINANT).** The prior library held three postures: tonal-ramp (neutral UI tinted by role), category-color (neutral page with per-category tint), and thin-semantic (near-monochrome app chrome). All three keep hue *timid relative to a neutral ground*. This source is the first where the dominant surface is itself a bold brand hue. The palette stays deliberately tiny — brand-hue + near-black (photographic/product panels) + paper-white (content) + ONE high-contrast accent for action — so the two-tier primitive→semantic spine and the "color is a scarce signal" discipline hold unchanged; only *how* hue is spent changes.

**Three structural consequences of a dominant chromatic ground:**
1. *Color-blocking becomes the composition lever* — the section boundary IS a color-field boundary, so alternating fields do the rhythm work that dividers/whitespace do on a neutral ground.
2. *Dual-ground foreground pairs become first-class* — because inverse fields cover ~half the page, semantic tokens must be authored as ground-aware pairs (text-on-light / text-on-brand, accent-on-light / accent-on-brand) from the start, not as a `-on-solid` edge suffix.
3. *The accent is chosen for chromatic distance* — a brand-hue button inside a brand-hue field vanishes, so the action color must escape the field by near-complementary separation, not merely by being "the lone saturated thing."

**Page composition — surface-weight as a second oscillation axis.** Prior sources oscillated density on HEIGHT because their surfaces were uniformly light. Here a short band on a full-saturation surface reads as *heavy*, so the method alternates light-surface and saturated-surface bands exactly as it alternates tall and short. Threaded proof rides the reserved accent (not a dedicated light surface); the grid-break-narrowing can be doubly marked by inverting the surface behind the narrowed measure — but the narrowing, not the color, stays the load-bearing act.

**Component architecture — entitlement as orthogonal content axes + two altitudes of reuse.** One parameterized app-shell is driven by a **view×role(×plan-tier) access matrix** rather than N hand-built sidebars; coverage is provable because every cell is decided. A second altitude of reuse sits above the atom library: **named, counted block collections** (contact sheets of pre-composed whole sections, e.g. "Data Tables — 8") whose count is a coverage checklist. Containers compose from **named slots** (shown as placeholders) filled by instance-swap, and reused primitives are **path-namespaced and serve double duty** as both documentation chips and live UI so the spec can't drift from the token.

**Page anatomy — richer skeleton catalogue.** Adds a standardized Section-Header organism (eyebrow?/heading/support?+action?, slots toggled by hidden flags), a conversion-ribbon trust band, a two-phase PDP (decision band → evidence narrative), distinct Order-Confirmation receipt with a loyalty-earned slot, and three non-standard page families most catalogues miss: a **guided quiz funnel** (Start → question steps → personalized results page), a **self-service RMA drawn as three state frames** (Enabled/Disabled/Success), and **slide-outs as first-class templates** with {header/body/pinned-action}.

### Register, not refutation
Nothing here overturns the existing spine. Every prior load-bearing move survives; what the source proves is that **register selects how each move is amplified and signalled**, not whether it applies. It should be read as the evidence that promotes "register-selected color method" and "two altitudes of reuse" from hypotheses to confirmed tools.
